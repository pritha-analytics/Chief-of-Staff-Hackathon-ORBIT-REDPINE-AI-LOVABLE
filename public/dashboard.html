    From: Sofia Bergström, CEO — NEXUS AI
    Subject: Re: AI Infrastructure Reliability — NEXUS AI Statement
    Hi Emma,

    Best regards,
    Sofia Bergström
    CEO, NEXUS AI

    Why each part is worded this way

    Why "subset" and not 67%
    "67% affected" becomes the headline instantly and destroys investor and customer confidence. "Subset" acknowledges the issue honestly without handing Emma the exact number she needs.
    Why mention engineers refused shortcuts
    Reframes from "NEXUS is broken" to "NEXUS engineers stood up for integrity." Emma may actually lead with this — it is a far better story for her too.
    Why not confirm Volvo
    If Emma writes "NEXUS confirmed Volvo" — Volvo reads it before Sofia calls. Unrecoverable. Never confirm specific customer names to press under any circumstances.
    Why offer a call
    A CEO who offers to speak directly signals confidence and control. It gives Sofia a chance to further shape the narrative. Emma is more likely to use the integrity angle if she speaks to Sofia personally.
    Why the closing reframe
    "A story about integrity, not failure" is the exact headline you want Emma to use. Give her a better story than what the competitors are pitching her.
