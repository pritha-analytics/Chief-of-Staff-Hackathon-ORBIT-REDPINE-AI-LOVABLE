## The Dashboard — 7 Tabs

### 1. Threat Map
All 8 active threats visualised with urgency levels, color coding, and "NEW" badges showing which threats arrived latest. Interconnections shown so the CEO understands why one action can de-risk multiple threats simultaneously.

### 2. Board Prep *(new — added after Alert 5 and 6)*
Michael's 3 exact questions with Sofia's exact answers. What to bring to the 8 AM board call. What NOT to say. Built specifically to help Sofia walk into the board room with surgical precision — exactly what Michael demanded.

### 3. Decision Matrix
Three strategic options with probability-weighted outcomes:
- **Option A** — Full transparency + offline (70% trust recovery, 30% revenue hit)
- **Option B** — 48-hr patch — **ELIMINATED** (SOC 2 violation, legally impossible)
- **Option C** — Hybrid containment — **RECOMMENDED** (80% controlled recovery)

### 4. 48-Hour Protocol
Interactive Gantt chart with 7 swimlanes — CEO, CTO, Head of Research, Customer Success, Comms, Legal/HR, Investor Relations. Click any cell to expand detailed bullet-point actions. Red glow cells = most urgent right now.

### 5. Comms Plan
Full sequenced communication strategy — who to contact, in what order, at what time. Includes the critical sequence warning: **Secure Okonkwo tonight → Pull VP Sales logs → Board call 8 AM → Emma reply → Volvo call.**

### 6. CEO Script
4-part exact script for Sofia:
- **Part 1** — Engineering team (confirm patch dead, secure retrain commitment)
- **Part 2** — Dr. Okonkwo retention call (most important call of the crisis)
- **Part 3** — Michael's board call (5 exact sentences, opens with apology)
- **Part 4** — Volvo call (4 sentences, Sentence 3 warns about press coverage)

### 7. Emma Reply
Sofia's complete email to TechCrunch journalist Emma — word for word. Every key phrase highlighted in blue. Full explanation of why each section is worded that way, so Sofia can defend every word choice.

## The Core Insight

> *Two calls tonight unlock everything — secure Dr. Okonkwo first, because without her the fix collapses. Then prepare Sofia for Michael's board call at 8 AM.*

Every decision in this plan flows from one question:

**Which single action, if taken tonight, stops the most damage at once?**

Not the product fix. Not the investor. Not the journalist. It is the scientist — Dr. Okonkwo. Because without her, the 3-week retrain is impossible. Without the retrain, there is no answer for the investor. Without an answer for the investor, Sofia loses her job.

---

## Key Decisions Made

**Why Option B was eliminated:**
The 48-hr patch required disabling audit logging — which violates SOC 2 certification. This is not a technical preference. It is a legal constraint. The CoS job was to tell Sofia: this option does not exist.

**Why the engineering team was addressed first:**
Maya's public #engineering Slack post was the confirmed leak source. Before any external communication, the leak had to be stopped. Engineers also needed to commit to the retrain — without them, no fix is possible.

**Why the Emma email never confirms 67% or $40-60M:**
Confirming those numbers hands Emma the headline. "Subset" acknowledges the issue without giving her the specific figure she needs for a damaging story.

**Why Sentence 3 in the Volvo script warns about press:**
Volvo must never read about themselves in TechCrunch before Sofia calls them. Telling them proactively about the press coverage is what keeps the relationship alive.

**Why the closing line of Emma's email is:**
*"A company that finds a problem, fixes it the right way, and tells its customers directly before telling the press — that is a story about integrity, not failure."*
Because that is the headline we want Emma to write. We gave it to her.
